The way from an agent account added to one at work is worded again (ADR-0094). "Add an agent account" is in three parts, who it is, the account it signs in with, and whom it reports to and where it starts; under the login it says what kind of name it is, an address at Google Workspace or a login at GitHub, and that the account need not be there yet and has to be before its machine is set up. Nothing on a page, in the command, in the guide or in the onboarding task says who makes an account at a service or what a service's terms ask: that is the organization's to say. Until its runner is heard from, an account's page shows what is next in order, the account, an invitation, the one line on its machine, its runner, each said done as the console sees it, in the pages' own words in English and Korean, where the lines of a service's module stood in English. The setup's guide has the agent ask the person at the machine for the sign-in before the command goes on, in words it can say as they are, and has a part of its own on signing in elsewhere in a browser: a browser tool attached to the machine's ordinary browser, where a sign-in stays, a person signing in inside the agent's session where one is wanted, and nothing the agent signs in with in a message. `console agent new` says the login and when it has to be there. For whoever builds a console: `AgentStartSteps`; `AgentInvitationPanel` takes `intro` and no longer `first`; `SetupGuideSection.key` gains `browser`; the words `agents.accounts.form.first` are gone and `agents.page.start` is new.
