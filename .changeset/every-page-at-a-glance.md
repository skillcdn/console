Every page reads at a glance: a section's title runs a line to the end, a page's head and a task's head are parted from what follows by a hairline, and what was on one line stands on its own. A project reads as its mark and name, its key under them, and its badges on a line of their own, with what waits in it under a line at the foot. A task in a list has its project under its title and its badges at the end. A run's head says the agent and its state on one line, with the way to end it at the end, and who it is for and since when on the next; a report's hour heads it small. A comment's hour stands at the end of its line. The task a decision is about is a chip. A goal's title stands alone over its badges and days. The facts beside a task stand in one column with a hairline between each; a token's facts and a category's key likewise have their place. A new token, `--sc-hairline`, is the line that parts things inside a pane.
