`console runner` (ADR-0085): the runner of an agent account, started in its desk on the command signed in as the account itself. It starts the agent's product as a child process, hands it what the console sends as messages under a header that carries a mark only the session knows, with what came from outside the console in a fenced block; holds what arrives together and delivers it as one message; keeps one session at a time and reports everything the product does as the session's transcript, in numbered batches, with every value of the account's secret store taken out first; and says every thirty seconds that it is there. It restarts nothing by itself: an agent the model refused, or whose product failed, waits, a supervisor's word is tried at once and what arrives by itself after a cool-down that doubles. A supervisor's `stop` holds until `restart`, which goes on with the conversation, or `reset`, which begins a new one. `runner.json` in the desk names the driver and the secrets a session is handed, as environment variables and as files under `work/`. The first driver is `claude`, over the product's streaming JSON input and output. The heartbeat's answer carries `delivered`. For whoever builds on it: the entry point `@skillcdn/console/runner` (`createRunner`, the `Driver` types, `createRunnerModules`, `composeSignals`, `redactorOf`, `parseRunnerConfig`), and `ConsoleClient.signalStream`.
