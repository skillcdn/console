A person's own decision is its project's (ADR-0077): once taken out of a private project, the people it is addressed to and the person who raised it no longer read it at its address, read its files or answer it, nor find it among their decisions, on their plate or in the count beside My decisions, where they went on doing all of it; listed again, they have it back. A plugin's decision reaches past its project as before, to those it is addressed to, who raised it and the managers above them. `ctx.console.decisions.answer` for a person answers a person's own decision only while they are in its project.
