The form of a SkillCDN token, a project's and the workspace's, says whose token it may be: one a person made on their account page for the pack's repository, or the one a deployment's operator made for a pack of the deployment's own directory, which a deployment of SkillCDN 0.5 may require of every reader. Nothing else changes: the console reads no meaning into a token, tries it against the pack once and keeps it sealed, as before. The skills `working-the-board` and `planning-the-board` say the same, and that no code connects such a token, since nobody signs in at a deployment that serves a directory.
