A plugin's pages run in a sandboxed frame the console's page embeds (ADR-0054, `docs/specs/plugins.md`, "Pages"): `PluginFrame` is the console's side of the bridge, performing a frame's calls under the plugin's own routes with the person's session and nothing else, and `PluginWidgets` the front page's grid of widgets; the tabs gain the plugins' groups (`NavItem.children`, `pluginNavItems`), a plugin with several pages its sub-tabs, and the addresses `/plugins/<key>[/<page>[/<rest>]]`, `/projects/<key>/plugins/<key>...` and `/settings/plugins[/<key>]` (`pluginHref`, `pluginSettingsHref`); the plugins' settings pages enable with consent, make or choose the board project, set the settings and the secrets, grant roles, show the events, export and purge. `CONSOLE_TOKENS` names the design tokens a frame is sent, with the new ones `--sc-color-info`, `--sc-color-neutral`, `--sc-series-1` to `-6` and `--sc-numeric`; the console's pages allow `frame-src 'self'`. The client gains `plugin(key).frameUrl`, `readFile`, `pluginEvents` and `purge`; the console's data carries `plugins` and a project's `projectPlugins`. A console answers `GET /api/v1/plugins/<key>/frame?page=|widget=`, `GET .../web/<version>/<path>` and `GET /api/v1/plugin-runtime/console.css`.
