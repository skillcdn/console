Every page is laid out alike, uses the page, and reads at a glance.

- The header and the tabs span the window on every page, so that the mark, the tabs and the person's menu stay where they are between the board and the other pages. Every page keeps the same margin at its sides, 3rem (`--sc-gutter`, new; as before on a narrow screen), and starts at the same height under the header, a page's head aligning its title and its ways on the baseline; the reading pages start where the board starts, their text still held to `--sc-width-prose`; the board page keeps as much room below its columns as above them. The room for a scrollbar is kept on every page (`scrollbar-gutter: stable`), so that the header does not shift between a page that scrolls and one that does not.
- A section's title runs a line to the end, a page's head and a task's head are parted from what follows by a hairline, and what was on one line stands on its own: a project reads as its mark and name, its key under them and its badges on a line of their own, with what waits in it under a line at the foot, the mark hanging in the margin where the rest wraps; a task in a list has its project under its title and its badges at the end, the people at work on it staying at the end of the row when the badges wrap; a run's head says the agent and its state on one line, with the way to end it at the end, and who it is for and since when on the next, a report's hour heading it small; a comment's hour stands at the end of its line; the task a decision is about is a chip; a goal's title stands alone over its badges and days; the facts beside a task stand in one column with a hairline between each; a project's skills are cards two abreast, each with its name, its folder, its words and its address; a goal's facts are cells with the name over the value; a task's subtasks and links, and what a run handed in, are rows on sheets, the state of a subtask at the end of its row; a page in a list reads on two lines, its title and under it its path, version, writer and time; the versions of a page are rows with the number, the title and who wrote it when; who wrote a page is labelled without a colon.
- The feed takes the page's width; the decisions put those waiting and the record side by side where the window is 64rem or wider; the roadmap's goals are cards two or more to a row; under the wide layout a task reads its text first, then its facts and the ways to its forms, then the discussion and what happened.
- On a card of the board the task's number stands on a line of its own above the title, small and in the code face; a column is headed at the reading size rather than in small capitals, and its count is a quiet grey pill on the column.
- The people of the organization and the members of a project are cards in a grid (`.sc-people`, columns of at least 18rem; the markup is as it was): the person on the first line, with "(you)" beside the name (`.sc-person-you`) rather than within the account line, the account and where it came from on the second, what they are and the ways to their work on the third, the member's role line small. In the header the live dot sits by the person's menu, whose picture is a little larger.
- The facts on a settings page, a project's and the workspace's, read as a table: a row per fact with its name on the left and its value beside it, a hairline between rows, the name over the value where the window is narrow.
