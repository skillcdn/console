A `reset` of an agent account gives what its product was handed and never took one more try, in the new conversation, and no more than one. What a product does not start with is kept and sent again, which is right for a sign-in that was mended; a signal the product could never start with was sent again with every signal after it, through every restart and reset, until someone ended the runner on its machine. Where the product does not start with it again after a reset it is let go: the session's record says which signals, the runner says so in a line, and whoever sent it says it again where it still matters. What a `restart` keeps is as before.
