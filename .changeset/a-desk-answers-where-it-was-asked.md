The desk an agent account is set up with says where an answer goes: at the messenger or on the board where the question came from there, and in the session for a supervisor's tell. A turn answered elsewhere ends in the session on one line of what was done, instead of the same answer a second time, so the transcript reads as a record and the conversation fills more slowly. A desk written before keeps its `AGENTS.md`: its account adds the sentence itself, under "Where I work".
