A card held near the board's edge carries the board on. While a card is dragged, the board scrolls on every frame the pointer is within the edge, with a mouse or a finger, still or moving, the nearer the edge the faster, as an icon held at the edge of a phone's screen turns the page; before, the board moved only as the pointer did, so that a card held still at the edge went nowhere. `edgeScrollOf` meters it and is exported from the board's module for a board of your own.
