A runner that did not run for a while says so to its agent: from when to when, how long, that the machine was asleep, most likely, and to tell whom the agent reports to. It finds the stretch by the clock alone, on macOS, Linux and Windows alike, where the clock is a minute or more past a timer of its own, and says it once, ahead of the next message the agent is handed, under the runner's header; stretches that follow one another before a message are said together. It begins no turn for it, and writes a line of its own for whoever reads the runner's output. `RunnerOptions` takes `moment`, which writes a moment with its day and is left out for ISO 8601 in UTC; `console runner` writes one as the command prints any moment.
