Breaking: the console holds its plugins (ADR-0049, ADR-0050, `docs/specs/plugins.md`): `GET /api/v1/plugins` lists them with their state and the asker's roles, `GET /api/v1/plugins/<key>` is one with its commands, queries, settings and, to an administrator, its consent, projects and grants, and an administrator enables (`POST .../enable`, with consent to every capability, the board project to make or choose, the projects, the settings), disables, configures (`PATCH .../settings`), seals secrets (`PUT`/`DELETE .../secrets/<name>`) and grants roles (`GET`/`POST .../grants`, `DELETE .../grants/<id>`); the shapes are `restPluginSchema`, `restPluginsSchema`, `restPluginDetailSchema`, `restPluginGrantSchema` and the inputs, the routes `REST_ROUTES.plugins` and `pluginPath`, the states `PLUGIN_STATES`. Every event names the plugin that caused it in `plugin` (`null` for the console's own), and eight kinds are new: `plugin.enabled`, `plugin.disabled`, `plugin.configured`, `plugin.granted`, `plugin.revoked`, `plugin.upgraded`, `plugin.command` and `plugin.failed`, with their words in the feed in English and Korean; `plugin.failed` is also a refusal (500), what a plugin's code throws that is not its own refusal. The floors move to 0.4.2 on both sides (ADR-0038, ADR-0043): an older command would refuse an event of a kind it does not know, so a console from 0.4.2 takes commands from 0.4.2, and this command needs a console from 0.4.2, which answers `plugin` on every event; the REST snapshot is taken again at them.
