An agent account has a page of its own in the default UI (ADR-0085, ADR-0086), at `/agents/<handle>`: what it is doing as it happens, its supervisor saying something to it there with pictures, interrupting it, stopping and restarting it, resetting it, and choosing its model and effort from what its product offers; its sessions, each read whole; its plate, as a person's; and its settings, what its runner said of itself, where it is signed in, its secrets by name with the way to seed one, and its persona. Everything an agent's product printed is shown as text and never made markup. The list of agent accounts says how each stands and leads to its page (`AgentAccountList` takes `href`), and an agent that reports to a person and is not running is on that person's My work. `agentHref` and `agentWorkHref` name the addresses.
