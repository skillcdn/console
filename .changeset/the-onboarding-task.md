An agent account's onboarding is a task (ADR-0088). When an account is made, the console makes its first task with it, "Set up @<handle>", in the project it starts in and with the account as its worker: what the agent does itself, and what only a person can do, to be asked as decisions of whoever can, in the words of the services the workspace names; a wake with the task's number waits on the account's record for its runner. `console agent new` says the task's number, and `POST /api/v1/agents` answers it as `onboarding`. The accounts an agent holds elsewhere are recorded on it (ADR-0089), by the account itself or a supervisor: `console agent <handle> --account github=acme-mira`, `=none` to take one away, `accounts` on `PATCH` and `GET /api/v1/agents/<handle>`; what it is called at each service and never a secret, shown on the account's Settings so that taking it out knows what to close. For whoever builds a console: `onboardingOf`, `onboardingTitle`, `onboardingWake`, `guideServicesOf`, `restAgentElsewhereSchema`, `MAX_AGENT_ELSEWHERE` and `MAX_ELSEWHERE_NAME_LENGTH`. The guide and the task ask nothing of a person at the service the account signs in through: that account comes before the line.
