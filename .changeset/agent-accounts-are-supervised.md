An agent account's supervisors are the administrators and the reporting line above it (ADR-0084): an agent of `GET /api/v1/agents` says `supervised`, whether the asker is one, and the listing answers the accounts the asker supervises beside the ones they see. Where a workspace narrows whom its members see to the people they share a project with, a member sees their own reporting line besides, whom they report to and whoever is below them. An agent account is never an administrator, the one rule of the policy that reads `kind`; on every other rule it stands where a person does.
