`console runner` says of its product only what the console takes. A model or a level of effort is letters, digits and `. _ : [ ] -`, up to 120 characters, and a product offers up to 50 of each: an agent over the Agent Client Protocol that offered more, or a model under a name with a slash, a space or an `@` in it, as a cloud provider names one, had every heartbeat refused, and the account turned `unheard` while it worked. A name in another shape is now left out of what is offered, what the session runs with is said as the product's own for it, a version that is no line is none, and the runner says in a line how many names it left out. `RUNNER_CHOICE` in the schemas is the rule both sides read.
