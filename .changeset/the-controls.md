The controls: a select (`select.sc-input`) draws a thin chevron like the browser's own, as far from its right edge as its words are from the left, and takes the room for it on its right; the chevron is a picture held in a new token, `--sc-select-chevron`, a grey stroke that reads on a light field and a dark one, which a console of its own may replace. A file field is as tall as any other field, with a button of the console's own to choose the file and the chosen file's name beside it, nothing of the browser's own file field seen; a page's attach form puts the file across, the name to call it under that and the attach button across the bottom. The ways under a comment's field stand at one height.
