`console runner` drives Codex too (ADR-0085): the `codex` driver, named by `"driver": "codex"` in the desk's `runner.json`. It runs the Codex binary without a terminal, `codex exec --json`, a process a turn, and goes on with the conversation by its id. The product takes no message while a turn runs, so a `tell` or a `wake` that arrives then is read when the turn ends, and an `interrupt` ends the turn's process first. With nobody at its terminal the product runs in its read-only sandbox, so the driver names `approve-for-me` where `runner.json` names no `permissions`: the desk may be written, and the product's own reviewer answers what would otherwise be asked of a person; `read-only`, `workspace-write` and `danger-full-access` are the other words. The models and the levels of effort a supervisor chooses from are the product's own list. Pictures are not handed to it yet. Run against the binary, 0.164.0, and not under the runner whole. For whoever builds on the runner: `createCodexDriver` in `@skillcdn/console/runner`.
