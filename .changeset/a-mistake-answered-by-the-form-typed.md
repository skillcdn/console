What a review of the command's new shape found, mended.

- **A word an object answers to by itself is no command.** `console constructor`, `console help toString` and `console task __proto__ --help` are answered as any word that is none, where the first ended with no sentence.
- **`console help version`** says what `console version` prints, and `console help help` is the table.
- **The pages name every flag their command takes**: `--file` on a supervisor's `agent wake`, `agent reset`, `agent interrupt`, `agent stop` and `agent restart` and on an account's own `agent wake me`, and `--run` and `--file` on `abandon`.
- **A form's flags stand before its words as after them**: `console doc write --title "Notes" guides/notes --body -` writes the page, as `console write` did, and an account's own `agent wake me` that was mistyped is answered with the options of its own form. A form that is typed as a flag has its page from `--help`: `console skills --search --help`, `console secret --accept --help`.
