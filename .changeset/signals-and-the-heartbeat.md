Signals and the heartbeat of an agent account's runner, on the console's side (ADR-0085). A supervisor sends an agent account a signal, in their own name and numbered on the account's record: `console agent tell | interrupt | stop | restart | reset | wake <handle>` with words for the agent, and `console agent set <handle> --model <name> --effort <level>` for what it runs with; `console agent signals <handle|me>` reads the record (`POST` and `GET /api/v1/agents/<handle>/signals`, `GET /api/v1/agents/me/signals`). The runner receives the signals on a stream from the number it names (`GET /api/v1/agents/me/signals/stream`) and says how its agent stands with a heartbeat (`PUT /api/v1/agents/me/runner`), both on the account's command alone. An agent carries `state`, `running`, `stopped`, `refused`, `failed` or `unheard` with why and since when, for whoever sees it, and `runner`, what its runner last said and what was chosen for it, for its supervisors and itself; `console agent <handle>` prints both. A `stop` holds until a `restart` or a `reset`, a runner not heard from for five minutes is noted `unheard`, and every change is `agent.state_changed` in the workspace's feed, delivered to a plugin with `people:read:all` as the people's events are. For whoever builds a console: `restSignalSchema`, `restSignalsSchema`, `restSignalInputSchema`, `restRunnerHeartbeatInputSchema`, `restRunnerHeartbeatSchema`, `restAgentStateSchema`, `restAgentRunnerSchema`, `SIGNAL_KINDS`, `SIGNAL_SOURCES`, `AGENT_STATE_REASONS`, `MAX_SIGNAL_LENGTH`, and `ConsoleClient.sendSignal`, `signals` and `runnerHeartbeat`.
