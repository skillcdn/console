A plugin's frame keeps to its policy. It could go to a file of its own bundle, where the frame's policy no longer held, and an SVG there ran scripts that reached anywhere and still spoke to the bridge; a bundle's files now carry a policy of their own and run nothing as a document, while a picture stays a picture. A frame holds no WebRTC either, which no policy governs: every script of a bundle is served with a line before it that takes WebRTC out of the frame and of any frame made inside it, so that a source map is a line out and an `integrity` attribute of a plugin's own no longer matches. A browser or a cache on the way keeps the scripts of a version it fetched before this, which are immutable, so that this reaches a plugin already installed with its next version.
