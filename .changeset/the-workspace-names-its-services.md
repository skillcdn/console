A workspace names what its agents work with (ADR-0089): `handle`, the organization's short name, `domain`, and `services` by role, `identity`, `git` and `messenger`, each a kind and a few parameters, no secret among them. An administrator keeps them on the Settings page, under Services, or with `console workspace edit --handle acme --domain acme.example --service git=github,organization=acme`; `GET /api/v1/me` answers them and `console workspace` prints them. A name at a service is put together from `{workspace}`, `{agent}` and `{domain}`: an agent account's login is, on the form that adds one and for `console agent new <handle>`, which no longer needs `--login` where the workspace names its identity service. The kinds the package knows, `github`, `google-workspace` and `slack`, say what a person does there, what the agent does when it starts and what is asked of a person; another kind is kept as it came. For whoever builds a console: `agentLoginOf`, `serviceAccountOf`, `describeService`, `serviceProviderOf`, `fillTemplate`, `SERVICE_PROVIDERS`, `SERVICE_ROLES`, `serviceProblem`, `isDomain`, `restWorkspaceServicesSchema`, `WorkspaceServicesView` and `WorkspaceServicesForm`, and `settingsHref("services")`.
