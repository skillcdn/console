An agent account's conversation reads as a chat. It stands in a pane of its own that fills the window under the account's head, follows its end while whoever reads is there, and offers the way back to the latest once they scrolled up; the box to speak in is one box at its foot, with the files chosen in it and no line on how the keys work. What a supervisor says stands in the conversation at once, as waiting until the agent takes it, with when it is read, and while a turn is under way the interrupt stands beside it, where the box had a button for it: it stops the turn and sends nothing twice. While a turn is under way the agent is seen at work, thinking, at a tool by its name or between the two, and a line says where a turn ended and how long it took. The steps of a turn, a thought and a tool's call, stand close together under what the agent said; who said a message stands under it; a picture of a session opens in the console's own overlay; an interrupt with no words is a note of who interrupted, and a message a session ended on unread says so. The words `agents.page.tell.keys`, `tellHint`, `tellHintAfterTurn`, `interrupt` and `interruptHint` are gone, and `agents.page.live` gains `interrupted`, `unread`, `turnDone`, `latest`, `latestNew`, `working` and `waiting`. The pane takes a scroll for the reader's own only after a wheel, a touch, a key or a press on it, so that it goes on following its end when the conversation changes how tall it is by itself; and a session is read once, when it is first shown, where the page read it again from its start every time it asked how the session stood and the count of its events had grown, which emptied the conversation under whoever was reading it. `followsAfter` takes whether the scroll was by hand in the place of where the pane stood, and `HAND_MS` is how long after a hand's touch a scroll counts as one.
