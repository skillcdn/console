The second pass of milestone 9: a project's mark, the calendar's bars, the listings a page at a time, and no claim that an agent is at work ([ADR-0020](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0020-the-console-does-not-claim-an-agent-is-at-work-a-run-says-when-it-was-last-heard-from-and-a-new-run-takes-over.md)). Additive, apart from the listings' answers, which now carry `more`, a run's `lastHeardAt` and a project's `color` and `symbol`, which every answer now carries, the refusal `run.task_taken`, which is gone, and the `runs` prop of `Board`, which is gone with the badge it fed.

- **The API client and the schemas.** A project carries `color` (one of `COLOR_NAMES`, or `null`) and `symbol` (up to `MAX_PROJECT_SYMBOL_LENGTH` characters with no whitespace, or `null`); `restProjectInputSchema` and `restProjectPatchSchema` take both, and a plate's `project` (`restWorkSchema`) carries them. `projectColorOf` and `projectSymbolOf` choose the mark the pages show where none is set: a colour from the key, never gray, and the first letter of the name. A project may carry an image of its own for the mark instead: `icon` (`{ sha256, contentType }`, `restProjectIconSchema`, or `null`), set with `client.project(key).setIcon(file)` and taken away with `removeIcon()`, read at `projectIconPath(key, icon)`; `PROJECT_ICON_TYPES` are the kinds allowed and `MAX_PROJECT_ICON_BYTES` the size. `useConsoleData`'s actions gain `setProjectIcon` and `removeProjectIcon`, and `createProject` takes the image as a second argument. `tasks()`, `decisions()` and `runs()` take `limit` and `offset` (`Page`) and answer `{ items, more }`, as `restTasksSchema`, `restDecisionsSchema` and `restRunsSchema` say; `DEFAULT_PAGE_LIMIT` is the command's page. A run carries `lastHeardAt`; `EVENT_KINDS` gains `run.taken_over`, since a run begun on a task takes over the one open on it. `COLOR_NAMES` and `ColorName` name the colours the pages paint; `CATEGORY_COLORS` is the same list.
- **The command.** `tasks`, `decisions` and `runs` take `--limit <n>` and `--offset <n>`, show `DEFAULT_PAGE_LIMIT` lines at a time, and end with `... more: --offset <n>` when there is more; `runs` says when each open run was last heard from; `take` takes over a run open on the task, and `help take` says so; `projects` counts no agents at work.
- **The default UI.** `ProjectMark` (a project's mark, `sm`, `md` or `lg`) on the projects page, in the header of a project's pages and on the rows of My work; `ProjectForm` takes a symbol and a colour, and an image, and shows the mark as it will be; `onSubmit` gets the image with the settings (a `File`, or `null` to take it away). `ProjectMark` shows the image when the project has one. `Calendar` draws a task planned across days as a bar from its start to its due day, in a lane of its own (`weekSegments`), with the colours of the `categories` given as its legend; the Calendar page is narrowed with `BoardFilters` as the board is, and `calendarHref(key, month, filter)` carries the filter with the month. `TaskList`, `EventFeed` and `DecisionLog` show `PAGE_SIZE` rows at a time with `ShowMore` (`usePaged` for a list of your own). `Board` takes no `runs` and a card names no agent; `RunCard` says when an open run was last heard from, and calls an open run open. The Categories page is in every member's navigation. The board no longer scrolls on its own below its columns. Both language packs changed accordingly.
