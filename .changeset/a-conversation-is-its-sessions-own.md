An agent account's page holds its conversation to the session shown, and says what it could not do. A text read whole is kept with the session it was read in, where it was kept by the event's number alone and so stood in place of the event of the same number once a restart or a reset began another session; the events before the first one shown are read once at a time and put in once. The box to speak in keeps its words and its files where the console refuses a tell or the request fails, with what went wrong under it, and is emptied once the tell was taken; before, it was emptied as it was sent. A session's stream that closed for good, as it does while the console restarts, is opened again from the last event read, a few seconds later and further apart while the console does not answer, and the conversation says at its end that the connection was lost once it has been for longer than a moment; before, the conversation stood still without a word until the page was loaded again. What the record names in a word of its own is said in the page's: the kind of a signal sent, what a tool's question was answered and the names of a note's facts read from the language pack, and a word the pack has none for stands as it came. Going from one account's page to another's begins a page of its own, with nothing of the first in the second's fields. `useAction`'s `act` answers whether the change went through.
