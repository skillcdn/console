A decision is addressed to a person (ADR-0042): whoever raises it, or the person an agent asks for, unless another person of the project is named, and the decision carries them as `decider`. My decisions, a page beside My work (`/decisions`), lists the decisions addressed to the person across their projects, the ones that wait first and then the answered as the record, and those the person asked of someone else, every row naming who asked and who is to answer, a page at a time (`MyDecisions`, `client.myDecisions`, `raised`); My work's decisions are those waiting for the person's own answer. `DecisionForm` takes `deciders`, the people who may be named, found by typing, and `me`, the default; a decision card says whom it is for where that is not the one who asked, and a waiting decision says whom it waits for. `console ask --to <login|me>` names the decider and `console decisions --mine` and `--raised` list one's own across the projects; `console work` says the decisions waiting for you. The REST API takes `deciderId` on a decision raised (`decision.invalid_decider` for a person who may not work in the project) and answers `GET /api/v1/decisions?open=&raised=&limit=&offset=`.
