The Agents page lists the workspace's agent accounts above a person's own agents (ADR-0084): each with its avatar, its name and what it does, its handle and the login it signs in with, whom it reports to, "Invited" until its first sign-in, and for its supervisors its picture to save as a PNG for its profile elsewhere, drawn in the browser. An administrator adds one at `/agents/new/account`: a few personas proposed and rolled again, the name, the handle and the title edited, the login it will sign in with, proposed from the handle and what the deployment's provider admits and edited freely, its manager, themselves unless another, and the project it starts in, with what a person does first at the provider said in plain words. `console agent new <handle> --login <login>` makes one with flags. A console that holds a token shows the agent accounts on its Agents page, and nothing of the tokens, as before. For whoever builds a console: `AgentAccountList`, `AgentAccountForm`, `suggestLogin`, `avatarPngBlob`, `ConsoleComponents.AgentAccountList` and `AgentAccountForm` to replace them, `ConsoleData.agents`, `createAgent` and `agentProposals` among the actions, `PATHS.newAgent`, and `createConsole`'s `persona.names`, which the form proposes from in place of the console's own.
