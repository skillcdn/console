A picture in a comment is a thumbnail that opens large over the page: zoom with the wheel, the buttons or two fingers, move with a drag, close with Escape, the close button or a click beside the picture, and the focus returns to the thumbnail. `PictureThumb`, `PictureOverlay`, `Lightbox` and `usePicture` are exported for a console of your own, and `Markdown` takes `onOpenPicture`.
