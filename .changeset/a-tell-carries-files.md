A `tell` and an `interrupt` carry files of any kind, up to eight (`MAX_SIGNAL_ATTACHMENTS`), where they carried four pictures (ADR-0095). A picture is still one by its bytes and the one kind the console shows in place; any other file is kept as what its sender called it and answered as bytes to save, and a file called a picture that is none is `application/octet-stream`. The runner writes every file of a signal in the agent's desk, at `work/inbox/<number>/<name>`, and the message says each on a line of its own after the signal's words: where it is, or that it could not be handed over. A picture is handed to the product as a picture too where the product takes one; where it takes none, the agent is told to open the file, and the event of what it was sent names the pictures the product was not shown (`unseen`) beside the other files (`files`). The `codex` driver takes pictures, each as `-i <path>`: a picture sent to an agent that runs Codex was left out unsaid before. For whoever writes a driver: `Picture.path`, `Driver.can.imageFiles`, `DriverSession.images()`, and a runner's `files.write` takes bytes; `attachedLine` and `deskName` beside `composeSignals`.
