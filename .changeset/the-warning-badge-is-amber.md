A warning badge is a pale amber pill with amber words and a clear amber line: its line was mixed from its words' colour, and a dark yellow line around a yellow fill under charcoal words read as a muddy brown outline. `--sc-color-warning` is an amber, `#a64b07`, at 5:1 or better on its fill and on the page, and `--sc-color-warning-subtle` a pale amber, `#fef3c7`; the words drawn in the warning's colour elsewhere take the same amber, and a warning callout's line is the badge's clear amber. A warning state keeps no line, as every state; a due badge keeps its own. A custom console that sets the two tokens itself keeps its own.
