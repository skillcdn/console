A warning badge is a pale amber pill with amber words and no line around it, whatever its kind: a dark yellow line around a yellow fill read as a muddy brown outline. `--sc-color-warning` is an amber, `#a64b07`, at 5:1 or better on its fill and on the page, and `--sc-color-warning-subtle` a pale amber, `#fef3c7`; the words drawn in the warning's colour elsewhere take the same amber, and a warning callout's line is a clear amber. A due badge keeps its line. A custom console that sets the two tokens itself keeps its own.
