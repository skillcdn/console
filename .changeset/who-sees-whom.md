Breaking: who may see whom is decided by one policy of the console, and the workspace keeps the settings it depends on (ADR-0035). `GET /api/v1/projects/<key>/members` answers, for a project open to the workspace, everyone active of the workspace, each with their role and `listed`, whether a listing holds them (`restMemberSchema`, optional, so that an older console still answers); `POST .../members` with a role of `member` is refused there as `member.open_project`, since everyone is a member already, and `PATCH .../members/<id>` makes an owner of anyone of the workspace, or takes an owner back to the member everyone is. `GET /api/v1/people` answers the people the asker may see, everyone or, when the workspace says `shared_projects`, the active people a member shares a project with, themselves and the administrators, the logins let in and the organization's reading going to whom it shows everyone; it takes `?login=` to find one person by their exact login, anyone active for an owner of a project, which `client.findPerson` reads and the command's `member`, `person` and `--assignee` use. `GET /api/v1/events` of the workspace is an administrator's while the people are narrowed. `PATCH /api/v1/workspace` changes the settings for an administrator (`restWorkspacePatchSchema`), the board hears `workspace.updated` (a new `EVENT_KINDS` entry) and `GET /api/v1/me` carries them in `workspace` (`restWorkspaceSchema`, `peopleVisibility` optional); `PEOPLE_VISIBILITIES` is the vocabulary. The pages: the Settings page has a Workspace section with the form at `/settings?workspace` (`WorkspaceSettingsView`, `WorkspaceSettingsForm`, `peopleVisibilityOf`), the Members page of an open project says so and shows everyone with an owner made from a row and nothing added or removed (`MemberList` takes `open` and `lookup`, a login input for an owner whose People page does not show everyone), the project form and settings say `Open: everyone of the workspace` or `Private: only those listed`, and the People page says what it shows. The command gains `console workspace` and `console workspace edit --people-visibility <everyone|shared-projects>`, and says how an owner is listed in an open project.
