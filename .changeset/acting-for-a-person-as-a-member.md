A plugin acting for a person acts with the person's standing as a member, never their administration (ADR-0072, amended by ADR-0076). `ctx.console.decisions.answer(id, answer, { for })` answers only a decision on the person's own plate, addressed to them or a plugin's addressed to someone they manage, up the reporting line, and is `plugin.forbidden` for any other, one they could answer only as a member of its project or as an administrator included; so that a mistake in matching a person elsewhere, or an unverified webhook, answers nothing that was never theirs. In a call made for a person (`ctx.plugins.get(key).for(personId)`), `ctx.console.people`, `teams` and `projects`, and the projects a call reaches, are the person's as a member, `projects:read:all` included. What a plugin another calls as itself writes on the board names no `via`, since it acts for nobody. `ctx.console.people.granted(role)` lists, for a role that follows the reporting line, whoever was granted it besides those who manage someone. `GET /api/v1/plugins/<key>` answers `asks` to an administrator alone, a role the plugin holds as the callee's `defaultRole` standing as granted. An id from plugin code that is not one (`for`, a decision's or a run's id, a task filter's `goal` or `worker`), and a task filter's `state` or `dueBy` that is not one, are refused before the database is asked, as nothing found or as `plugin.invalid_args`, where they failed the call. `createTestContext` takes `console.decisions.answer`, which it dropped.
