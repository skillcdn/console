Who is in is the console's own record, and an administrator keeps it (ADR-0022).

- **A person has a standing.** Every person carries `status` (`active` or `deactivated`), `source` (what let them in: `organization`, `domain` or `listed`) and `deactivatedAt`. A deactivated person is kept with everything they did, refused on every request with their agents, and offered to no picker. `PERSON_STATUSES`, `PersonStatus`, `MEMBERSHIP_SOURCES` and `MembershipSource` name the words; `vocabulary.status` says them.
- **The REST API.** `GET /api/v1/people` answers `listed` beside `items`: the logins an administrator let in that nobody has signed in with yet. `PATCH /api/v1/people/<id>` takes `status` beside `role` (`person.of_organization` for a person the organization let in; `person.last_admin` keeps one administrator in). `POST /api/v1/listings` lets a login in (`listing.exists`, `listing.unknown_host`) and `DELETE /api/v1/listings/<id>` takes one away (`listing.not_found`). The workspace's feed gains `person.listed`, `person.unlisted`, `person.deactivated` and `person.reactivated`.
- **The client.** `client.createListing(input)`, `client.removeListing(id)`, `restListingSchema`, `restListingInputSchema`, `RestListing`, `RestListingInput`, `restPath("listings", id)`, `REST_ROUTES.listings`, `hostOfLogin(login)` and `isLoginAt(host, login)`, `MAX_LOGIN_LENGTH`.
- **The command.** `console people` lists the deactivated as such and the logins let in; `console person add <login> [--host gh|google]`, `person remove <login>`, `person deactivate <login>` and `person reactivate <login>`.
- **The default UI.** The People page keeps those in, those let in and not signed in yet, and the deactivated apart, with the ways an administrator lets someone in (`/people/new`, the route `new-listing`, `PATHS.newListing`), takes a person out or back in, and takes a listing away. `PeopleList` takes `listed`, `onChangeStatus` and `onUnlist`; `ListingForm` is the form; `useConsoleData` answers `listed` and the actions `createListing` and `removeListing`. New classes: `sc-people-page`, `sc-people-section`, `sc-person-note`, `sc-person-row-out`, `sc-listing-form`, `sc-field-hint`. Words under `people`: `letIn`, `letInAria`, `notSignedIn`, `deactivated`, `deactivate`, `reactivate`, `leftOrganization`, `source`, `at`, `form`; under `feed.event`: `personListed`, `personUnlisted`, `personDeactivated`, `personReactivated`.
- **The deployment.** `MEMBERS` is gone: who may sign in is the People page's, the Workspace domain's and, with the GitHub App, the organization's; `ADMINS` lets its logins in and makes them administrators at sign-in.
