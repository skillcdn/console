An agent account keeps its secrets in the console (ADR-0087): named values, sealed with the deployment's key for their account and their name, which the account alone reads back. The account, signed in as itself, has `console secrets` (the names), `console secret <name>` (the value, and nothing else), `console secret <name> --set` (typed unseen, or from `--file`, with `--expires` for a value that ends on a known day) and `--remove`; a token the account made for an agent of its own reads none of them. A supervisor has `console agent secrets <handle>` and `console agent secret <handle> <name> [--remove]`: a secret seeded write-only, so that a person or an agent above hands the account a credential it cannot make for itself, and never read back. Every setting, reading and removal is on the account's record, by name, read by its supervisors (`GET /api/v1/agents/<handle>/secret-accesses`), and none is an event of the feed. A handle is none of the words that follow `console agent` in the command (`secret`, `tell`, `stop` and the like), beside the words of its addresses. For whoever builds a console: `restAgentSecretSchema` and its kind, `isSecretName`, `SECRET_ACCESSES`, the bounds `MAX_AGENT_SECRET_NAME_LENGTH`, `MAX_AGENT_SECRET_BYTES` and `MAX_AGENT_SECRETS`, and `ConsoleClient.secrets`, `secret`, `setSecret`, `removeSecret`, `agentSecrets`, `seedAgentSecret`, `removeAgentSecret` and `agentSecretAccesses`.
