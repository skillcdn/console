Breaking: a task has any number of workers in place of one assignee (ADR-0037). On the wire a task carries `workers`, the people at work on it in the order they came to it, instead of `assignee`; `POST` and `PATCH .../tasks` take `workerIds`, every worker in place of those there were, `[]` for nobody, up to `MAX_WORKERS`, instead of `assigneeId`; the listing is narrowed by `worker=` instead of `assignee=`, and `unassigned=true` keeps the tasks with no worker; the refusal is `task.invalid_worker`; `task.updated` names the field `workers`; a run taking a task puts its person among the workers, beside those there were. `TaskFilter.assignee` is `worker`. The migration moves each task's assignee into `task_workers` and keeps the column, unused, until the next release.

A listing is searched by the words of a title: `GET .../tasks?q=`, every word in the title whatever the case, a number with or without `#` finding the task of it; and `GET .../goals?q=`, the roadmap being paged now (`limit`, `offset`, `more`) and narrowed by `open=`. `TaskFilter.q`; `GoalFilter` with `q`, `open` and a page. The command: `console tasks --search <words>`, `console goals --search <words> [--limit <n>] [--offset <n>]`, and `console task new|edit --worker <login|me|none>`, repeated or with commas between, in place of `--assignee`.

The pages: `TaskForm` takes `people` as the workers to choose from, and `findTasks` and `findGoals`, searches a page at a time by which the task it is part of and the goal it works towards are found, in place of `parents` and `goals` whole; `Picker` is the component that searches, `PICKER_PAGE_LIMIT` its page; `TaskView` takes `parent`, `findTasks` and `findGoals`; `Workers` shows the people at work on a task as pictures side by side; `workloadOf` counts a task for each of its workers; `useConsoleData` offers `findTasks` and `findGoals` among its actions and reads the goals page after page. The Korean pages say 작업자 for what was 배정.
