An agent account carries a mark after its name wherever a member is named short: "AI" in a small frame, with "Agent account" on hover, on a task's owner and workers, on who a decision is for and who raised or answered it, on a comment's author, a manager, a member of a project or a team, and among the people a form offers to choose from. Where only pictures stand, the workers on a card, the names on hover say it ("Mira (AI)"). A person carries nothing. For whoever builds a console: `KindMark` is the part, `PersonChip` and `PersonOption` carry it, and the words are `people.kindMark.agent` and `agentTitle`.
