The directory keeps a work email ([ADR-0069](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0069-the-directory-keeps-a-work-email-read-by-an-administrator-the-person-and-a-plugin-allowed-to.md)). A person who signs in at Google has the address Google vouched for, written again at each sign-in; an administrator writes everyone else's, on the People page or with `console person email <login> <address|none>` (`PATCH /api/v1/people/<id>` with `email`, lowercase, one per workspace, `person.email_taken` otherwise). A person carries `email` (read as optional) to an administrator and to themselves alone, on `GET /api/v1/people` and `GET /api/v1/me`; everywhere else a person is named without it. A plugin reads it with the new capability `people:read:email`, "the work email of the people it may read, to match them to accounts elsewhere", on `ctx.console.people.list` and `get`; `console people` prints it in angle brackets where the console answers one. The event `person.email_changed` carries the login and whether an address is set, never the address, and reaches the hooks of a plugin that reads the directory. `PeopleList` takes `onChangeEmail`.
