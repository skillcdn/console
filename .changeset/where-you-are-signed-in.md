A person sees the browsers they are signed in on, on the Agents page: each as a browser on a platform, where it last came from and when it was last used, the one they look from marked; and signs any other out, or every other at once. `GET /api/v1/sessions`, `DELETE /api/v1/sessions/<id>` and `DELETE /api/v1/sessions/others`, for a person on the console's own pages and never for a token; `SessionList` is a component of the package, and the client has `sessions`, `endSession` and `endOtherSessions`. A session begun before this names no browser.
