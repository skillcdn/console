The People page reads at a glance, and the teams have a view of their own. A person's card lists their facts in one column of names and one of values: their role, manager and work email, which an administrator changes in place in quiet fields that read as values until the pointer or the focus is on them; whom they manage, as faces with how many; and their teams, as chips with the way to add one at the end of the row. The teams are a view of the People page beside the people's, `/people/teams`, with the form that makes one at `/people/teams/new`: a card per team with how many are in it and who, its name written anew in its place, a person added from the team's own card and taken out from their chip, and the way to delete a team, asked first, since the roles granted to it go with it. `TeamList` and `TeamForm` are exported, and `TeamList` is among the components a console may replace; `PeopleList` keeps the teams of a person (`teams`, `onJoinTeam`, `onLeaveTeam`) and no longer the teams themselves.

`Chip` is a new block: a thing named among others in a row, a person, a team, an item of a list, a holder of a role, with the way to take it out where it can be. The workers of a task, the people a decision is for, a list setting of a plugin and the holders of a plugin's role are rows of it, chips are low pills of one shape wherever they are (`.sc-chip`, the holders of a role `sc-chip sc-plugin-holder`), and the way to add one more stands at the end of the row as a chip, a `Picker` with the new `className` `sc-picker-add`; `sc-picker-quiet` is a picker that reads as what it holds, as `.sc-input-quiet` is now for any field, a select or an input. A choice of a list setting is a pill lit in the accent with a tick. The words read better: a secret the console received says from which host, and until when; the time zone nobody set, the role that follows the reporting line and the board's filter of people read plainly in Korean.
