A wake carries what the agent's conversation does not hold, and says it once (ADR-0166).

- **After a restart of the runner nothing is said again.** What the `slack` wake source brought the agent is kept in a file of its own under the desk's `work/` (`runner-kept-slack.json`: ids and moments, never a word), read when the runner starts and held to its shapes there. That file and the runner's state are written beside themselves and then given their names, so that a write cut short leaves the file there was.
- **After a reset the first wake of a conversation carries what led up to the message.** The runner says when the agent's conversation began (`WakeHost.began`, kept in its state as `began`): at a reset, with a new day's conversation, where a product asked to go on with a conversation began one in its place (`conversation` with `anew`, which the `acp` and `codex` drivers say), and where the product put the conversation in fewer words (`DriverEvent` has `compacted`, which the `claude` driver says). The first wake of a place in a conversation begun since carries the last five messages before it, the agent's own as `from you`.
- **Earlier messages share what a wake holds of them**: one alone up to 2,000 characters, three 800 each, each of five and more 500, where every one was cut at 500. One of many short lines is cut shorter until it fits, and not left out.
- **A wake's first line says more.** A message of a channel in no thread reads `outside any thread: a reply begins the thread ...`, where it read `in the thread ...` as a reply does. A message among several that names others and not the agent says `naming U0456WXYZ and not you`; nothing of it where the message calls a group of members, which the agent may be in. A file is named with its id, `[file notes.pdf (F0123ABCD), not carried in this wake]`. What to judge by whether to answer is said once for a conversation or a thread, and the line names the others and the command after that.
- **A supervisor's message with a file says that a supervisor wrote.** Such a message names no workspace for its writer; in a conversation the messenger says is shared with no other organization, the agent's own is taken for it.
- **After a usage limit, what the conversation holds is named and not said again**: one line with the numbers of the signals the product had taken, ahead of what reached the agent since. Only in the session whose product took them, and only under a driver that vouches that what it said was taken stands in the conversation (`can.holdsTaken`, which the `claude` and `codex` drivers say): a session begun after, and the `acp` driver, say them whole, as before.
- **A wake for a comment carries the comment**, in a block, as its writer's words; a long one is cut, and the line says so. A console of this version sends it, and a runner of this version reads it in parts; an older runner prints the text as it is kept, the comment's lines behind `>`.
- **A wake an account kept for itself, and its own reset, read `yourself`** in the header.

For whoever builds on the package: `RunnerOptions.wakes.kept` reads and writes what a source keeps; a driver of your own says `can.holdsTaken` where what it reports as taken stands in its product's conversation, and `conversation` with `anew` where it began a conversation in place of the one it was asked to go on with; `composeSignals` takes `own` and `above`; `wakeTextOf`, `wakePartsOf` and `WakePart` are exported from `@skillcdn/console/api` too.
