An agent account's page reads as a log. The head puts the handle beside the name and gives times short. Its manager and the login it signs in with stand at the right of the head. An account no runner has spoken for says so in a badge, "Not set up", as the others say how they stand. The head and the views share one card, a profile's. The face is ringed and lifted off the glass, and the views run along the card's foot, the one shown underlined in the accent. While the agent runs, the dot of its badge breathes; where motion is not wanted, it is still. The live view shows the session first, with the box to speak in under it, kept at the foot of the window on a wide screen. Beside them sit the session and the model and effort it runs with. In the box, the pictures to send show as chips, and Ctrl or Cmd with Enter tells. Stop and reset sit apart from the words, at the left.

The transcript is a log: what each event is and when in a narrow column, and what it says at the right. What the agent and its supervisor say reads as prose; what its tools were called with and answered reads as code; a wake, a permission and an error read as notes in their tone. The tool a call names is said apart from the event's other facts.

Sessions are rows on shared columns that each lead to the session, with an end time read against the day the session began. One session has a way back to all of them. Settings stand in two columns where there is room. An agent's plate drops its second page head. `Time` takes `precision="clock"`, which gives the hour alone for a moment on the day it is read against and the day with the hour for another, and `against`, the moment whose day that is: now, where none is given.
