A project on the front page comes at a glance of its board too, as `medium` 4×2, `large` 4×4 and `wide` 8×2 (ADR-0105): its mark, its name and three figures, the tasks in progress, those in review and the decisions waiting; under them, or beside them on a wide tile, its tasks in progress, the soonest due first, its goals ahead with their days and their bars, and its tasks waiting for review. The rows are fitted to the room the tile has, each whole, with how many more there are, and parted by hairlines. A glance is read in one request (ADR-0107): `GET /api/v1/projects/<key>/glance`, `client.project(key).glance()`, answers the board counted by state, the decisions waiting and the rows, ordered and cut by the console with how many there are in all (`RestProjectGlance`, with `restProjectGlanceSchema`, `RestGlanceTask`, `RestGlanceGoal` and `GLANCE_ROWS`), so that a tile's figures and its rows are of one moment. `HomeGrid` takes the answers as `glances`, by the project's id, and asks for them through `onGlance` with the projects it shows at a glance, an empty list when it shows none; `ProjectTile` takes one as `glance` (`ProjectGlance`). `createGlanceReader` reads them for a composition of your own: once for each reading, again when asked, a failed read tried again and a late answer dropped. `glanceFit`, `glanceProjects`, `isGlanceSize` and `GLANCE_REM` are exported with them. The default console reads the glances each time the front page is shown and when the person comes back to its window, and the projects with them (`actions.refreshProjects()` of `useConsoleData`). `ProgressBar` given `words={false}` is the bar alone.
