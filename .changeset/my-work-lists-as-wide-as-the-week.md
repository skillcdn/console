My work's lists stand as wide as the week above them: two side by side where each still has a reading width, one under the other below that, every list as wide as the others so that their rows line up. They kept to a reading width alone, which left the right of the page empty under a week drawn across it. `.sc-work-lists` lays them out; a custom console that set its `max-width` keeps it.
