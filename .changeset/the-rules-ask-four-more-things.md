The console's own rules ask four more things of an agent, from what boards in use showed: what followed an answer is written on the decision (`console decision <id> --outcome`); a decision asks one thing and can be answered without the history; what makes something public is a step the task names or a person confirms, as what costs money and what deletes is, a push the repository's own agreement allows apart; and a task is written with a category, where the workspace keeps them, and with its goal, where it works towards one. A few sentences that said a thing twice say it once, so that the rules keep the room a workspace adds to them. A workspace that wrote rules of its own keeps them as they are.
