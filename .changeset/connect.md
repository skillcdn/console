An agent connects without anyone copying a token (ADR-0011). `console login --url <origin>` shows an address and a short code and waits; a person opens the address signed in, names the agent and approves on the new `/connect/<code>` page; the command receives a token of its own, made then and handed over once, and never shows it. `--agent <name>` says what the agent calls itself; `--token-stdin` still takes a token made by hand, for a script. The Agents page says in plain words what to tell an agent, lists what is connected and disconnects it; an administrator sees and disconnects anyone's agents from the People page; a workspace may require of tokens an expiry at most, which the forms and `GET /api/v1/me` (`workspace.tokenDaysAtMost`) reflect. The client gains `connect`, `connectRequest`, `approveConnection`, `denyConnection`, `claimConnection`, `personTokens` and `revokePersonToken`; the components `ConnectCodeForm`, `ConnectApproval`, `ConnectWords` and `ConnectPage`; the schemas of the connection; `normalizeConnectCode`. `TokenForm` takes a `name`, `daysAtMost` and its words. The command's `readSecret` is gone from `CliIo`, and `hostname` is new.
