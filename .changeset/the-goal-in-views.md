A goal's page reads as the board does: its facts in a row at the head, and its tasks under a switch between the board of them, the list by state and the calendar (`?view=`, `?month=`), the board opening by default. A card is dragged by its grip to another column to move the task, on the project's board and a goal's alike, with a mouse or a finger; the select stays for the keyboard. `GoalView` takes `view`, `viewHref`, `board` and `calendar`; `goalHref` takes options in place of the form; `goalMonth`, `columnStateOf`, `GOAL_TASK_VIEWS` and `GoalTaskView` are exported.
