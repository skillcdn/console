The calendars tell a cut title at once. On a project's calendar and on the week of My work, a task whose title its line cut short, or a bar on a day where it shows no title, now shows the whole title in a tip of the console's own the moment the pointer is on it, or the item is focused, in place of the browser's tooltip that came after a wait. The items carry `data-tip` and the class `sc-tip`, and `data-clipped` while the tip applies; a bar no longer clips its own box, its title does.
