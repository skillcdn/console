The invitation that leads a machine to an agent account, and the guide it opens (ADR-0088). A supervisor makes it on the account's Settings, under "Its machine", or with `console agent invite <handle>`: a code of eight letters and digits, shown once, in the one line to say to the agent's product on its machine; made again it takes the place of the one before, and `--revoke` takes it away. The code is no credential: it is good for seven days or until the account's command has signed in, it says which account a setup is for, and the account is entered through its own sign-in alone. `console agent setup --console <origin> --code <code>` prints the guide of that setup on a machine signed in nowhere, and `/setup/<code>` shows the same guide to a person without a sign-in: what whoever prepares the machine does first, how the account signs the command in as itself, what the agent does when it starts and what is asked of a person, in the words of the services the workspace names. A new agent account's form leads to its Settings, where the invitation is made. For whoever builds a console: `client.agentInvitation`, `inviteAgent`, `revokeAgentInvitation` and `agentSetup`, `setupGuideOf`, `setupGuideText`, `setupCommandOf`, `setupSentenceOf`, `SetupGuideView`, `AgentInvitationPanel`, `setupHref`, the route `setup`, and `AgentPage` takes the `workspace` whose identity service says what a person does first.
