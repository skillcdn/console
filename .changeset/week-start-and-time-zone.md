The workspace keeps the day its calendars begin a week on and its time zone (ADR-0035): `weekStart` (`auto`, the system default, as the language and region of each person's device have it, or `monday`, `sunday`; `WEEK_STARTS`) and `timeZone` (an IANA name, or `null`) on `me.workspace` (`restWorkspaceSchema`, both optional for an older console) and in `PATCH /api/v1/workspace` (`restWorkspacePatchSchema`, a zone the console knows, `request.invalid` otherwise; `timeZoneName` is the shape). `firstDayOfWeek(language, weekStart)` takes the workspace's word, `deviceLanguage` is the language the system default follows, and `Calendar`, `Week` and `WorkList` take `weekStart`; `weekStartOf` and `timeZoneOf` read a workspace's. The Settings page's Workspace section and form show and change both, each setting a row with its words beside it, the zone picked by area with its offset (`timeZoneGroups`, `deviceTimeZone`, `offsetOf`), the device's offered. The command prints moments in the workspace's time zone, as ISO 8601 with its offset, as its login kept it at `login`, `whoami` or `workspace` (the credentials file gains `timeZone` on a login), and as the API gives them, in UTC, while none is known or with `--json`; `moment` and `setMomentZone` are the command's; `console workspace edit` takes `--week-start` and `--time-zone`.
