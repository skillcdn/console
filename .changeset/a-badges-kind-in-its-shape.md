A badge's kind shows in its shape, not only its colour: a category is the soft pill, a state carries a dot of its colour, a priority is outlined with nothing filled, a due day is a tag with square corners, and a count of decisions keeps the pill with its figures lined up. `Badge` takes a `kind`, which the state, priority, due and count badges set as `sc-badge-kind-*`.
