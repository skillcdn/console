The skills the package carries, read again against the authoring guide of the reference pack. `planning-the-board` is new, for the work a person puts on the board and the project they set up: tasks written from what they say or plan, with their days, categories, goals, parents and workers; goals on the roadmap; a project made or changed, with its members and its rules; the workspace's categories, people and settings; a private pack connected. It is split from `working-the-board`, which works one task to its end, reads the task in full before taking it, names the quotas and the refusal of a person who is out where a run meets them, and reads the project's pack as its reference `the-pack.md` says. Both descriptions say what comes out, then when to use the skill in the words people say, then which neighbouring skill to use instead; the manifest says what an instruction is and what is material.
