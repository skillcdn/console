The schedule, the roadmap and the categories ([ADR-0019](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0019-a-task-is-planned-in-days-the-board-keeps-its-moments-and-goals-and-categories-give-the-work-its-shape.md)): a task is planned in days and the board keeps its moments, tasks are sorted into the workspace's categories, a project has a roadmap of goals whose progress is read from their tasks, and the pages that follow. Additive, apart from the fields every task and every event now carry and a board route that now carries its filter.

- **The API client and the schemas.** A task carries `category` (its key, name and colour, or `null`), `goalId`, `startsOn`, `dueOn`, `startedAt` and `doneAt`; `restTaskInputSchema` and `restTaskPatchSchema` take `category` (a key), `goalId`, `startsOn` and `dueOn`. `tasks()` takes a `TaskFilter` (`state`, `open`, `category`, `goal`, `assignee`, `dueBy`). New on `client.project(key)`: `goals()`, `goal(id)`, `createGoal`, `updateGoal`; on the client: `categories()`, `createCategory`, `updateCategory`, `removeCategory`, `work()`. New schemas: `restCategorySchema`, `restCategoriesSchema`, `restCategoryInputSchema`, `restCategoryPatchSchema`, `restGoalSchema`, `restGoalsSchema`, `restGoalInputSchema`, `restGoalPatchSchema`, `restTaskCountsSchema`, `restWorkSchema`. The vocabulary gains `GOAL_STATES`, `CATEGORY_COLORS` and `OPEN_TASK_STATES`; `EVENT_KINDS` gains `category.*` and `goal.*`; an event carries `goalId`. New bounds: `MAX_CATEGORIES`, `MAX_CATEGORY_KEY_LENGTH`, `MAX_CATEGORY_NAME_LENGTH`. `REST_ROUTES` gains `categories` and `work`, `PROJECT_COLLECTIONS` gains `goals`, and `categoryPath(key)` is new.
- **The command.** `console work` (what is on your plate across your projects), `console tasks --open --mine --category --goal --due <day|today|week|overdue>`, `console task new` with `--category --goal --starts --due --assignee --parent`, `console task edit`, `console goals`, `console goal <id>`, `goal new`, `goal edit`, and `console categories`. `console task` says the plan and the moments.
- **The default UI.** The board's filters (`BoardFilters`, `filterTasks`, `byDue`; the address carries them, `boardHref`), the Calendar page (`Calendar`, `Workload`, `workloadOf`; `calendarHref`), the Roadmap page with a goal's own page and form (`GoalList`, `GoalView`, `GoalForm`; `goalHref`, `newGoalHref`), the workspace's My work page (`WorkList`) and Categories page (`CategoryList`), tasks as rows (`TaskList`), the decisions' record (`DecisionLog`), and the small blocks `CategoryBadge`, `DueBadge`, `GoalStateBadge`, `ProgressBar` and `Day`. `TaskForm` and `TaskView` take `categories` and `goals`; `Board` takes `runs` and names the agent at work on a card; `ConsoleComponents` names `Calendar`, `GoalList`, `WorkList` and `CategoryList`. The day helpers (`today`, `addDays`, `monthGrid`, `formatDay` and the rest) are exported. `useConsoleData` carries `categories` and `goals` with the actions on them. Both language packs grew.
