A select (`select.sc-input`) draws a thin chevron like the browser's own, as far from its right edge as its words are from the left, where the browser's arrow sat on the border; the select takes the room for it on its right. The chevron is a picture held in a new token, `--sc-select-chevron`, a grey stroke that reads on a light field and a dark one, which a console of its own may replace.
