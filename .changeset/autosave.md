`console runner` saves the desk at the end of every session (ADR-0088): where the desk is a git repository and `runner.json` leaves `autosave` on, everything the desk does not ignore is committed, as "Autosave at the end of a session", where anything changed, and pushed, a net under the agent's own commits. A step git refuses is said in the runner's line by how git ended, and the runner goes on. For whoever runs a runner of their own: `createRunner` takes `git`, and `AUTOSAVE_MESSAGE` is the commit's words.
