The My decisions tab says how many decisions wait for the person across the projects they may see, as a project's Decisions tab says of its own: `GET /api/v1/me` answers `decisions.waiting` (`null` for nobody; read as optional, for an older console), and the pages read it again when the person raises or answers a decision, or one is raised or answered on the project's feed.
