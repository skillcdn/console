A decision's files reach whom the decision reaches ([ADR-0068](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0068-a-decision-reaches-whom-it-is-addressed-to-wherever-it-is-and-a-plugins-reaches-the-managers-above-them.md)). The person a decision is addressed to outside its project, and for a plugin's decision a manager up the reporting line, opened it among their decisions and at `/decisions/<id>`, and its attachments and the pictures its context names answered `404` at the project's address for files. They are read at the decision's own address now, `GET /api/v1/decisions/<id>/files/<file id>`, for whoever the decision reaches, with a session or a token, served as the project's files are, and only the files of that decision; another file of the project and a decision out of reach are `file.not_found` alike. The client gains `decisionFileUrl(decisionId, fileId)`, and the routes `decisionFilePath`. `MyDecisions` reads a decision's files there where `fileHref` is left out, where it named them without a link, and gives a `fileHref` of a console's own the decision's id as a third argument; the page at `/decisions/<id>` reads them there too. A plugin command that raised a decision in a project other than its board says that project as where the decision waits (`project` on the invocation), and no project where the caller does not reach the decision.
