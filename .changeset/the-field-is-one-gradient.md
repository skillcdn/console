The field is one gradient, from the top down, and a pane's rim is a hairline. The shell paints `--sc-field-gradient`, a new token, under the far light, and the lights are off by default (`--sc-color-far`, `--sc-color-far-turn` and `--sc-color-vignette` transparent, `--sc-light-strength` 0), so that the page is even rather than blotched; a console that sets them keeps its lights as before. The rim of a pane is one pixel (`--sc-glass-lens-width`) of a softer white (`--sc-color-glass-edge`), and its sheen the light along the top edge only (`--sc-glass-sheen`).
