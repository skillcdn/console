Breaking: the front page is one grid of tiles a person arranges by hand ([ADR-0061](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0061-the-front-page-is-one-grid-of-tiles-a-person-arranges-by-hand.md)). The projects and the plugins' widgets stand side by side on square cells, 12, 8, 6 or 4 columns by the width. A project is an `icon` of 1×1 cells or `small` of 2×2. A widget comes in sizes of one family, `small` 2×2, `medium` 4×2, `large` 4×4, `wide` 8×2 and `full` 8×4, declared as `sizes` with a `defaultSize` in the manifest, at least one of them at most four cells across, for a phone ([ADR-0062](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0062-a-widget-comes-in-a-size-a-phone-can-hold.md)), the one `size` of before reading as one of them; it fills the cells its size gives it, and its frame is told the size it has on this grid in `init.size` and in a `size` message when it changes (`frame.context.size`, `frame.onSize` in `@skillcdn/console/plugin/web`). Edit layout opens nothing on the page: a tile is taken in hand with the mouse, or a finger that rests on it a moment first, and put down on any cell, those in the way moving aside; the arrow keys and Delete do the same from the keyboard, each tile carries the way off the page and the sizes it comes in, and Done keeps it all. There is no default layout: what is new takes the first room, a plugin's widgets as soon as it is on, and what was taken off stays off and is offered to add back. It is kept per width as `home` on `GET /api/v1/me` and `PATCH /api/v1/me`, read as optional. `HomeGrid` and `ProjectTile` take the place of `ProjectList` and `PluginWidgets`, in the package's exports and among the components a console is composed of. `MAX_PLUGIN_WIDGET_HEIGHT` is no longer read: the cells of a size bound a widget.
