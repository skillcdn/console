`console tasks --unassigned` lists the tasks assigned to nobody (`unassigned=true` on the REST listing, and `unassigned` on the client's task filter); `console member add <login> --project a,b,c` lists a person in several projects at once, one line per project, one listed already said so; and the console's own rules name what the person whose token the command holds says as an instruction, next to the task, the rules and the answers on the board.
