A decision that waits can be addressed anew (ADR-0096): the people named take the place of those it was for, so that a question an agent asked of the person it works for is passed to the colleague whose answer it is, and nothing is raised twice. On the pages, a waiting decision has Change who decides, wherever it is answered: its project's decisions, its task, My decisions and its own page. `console decision <id> --to <login|me>`, given once for each person, does the same from the command. Anyone who may work in the project may, as anyone may answer; an answered decision stays as it was addressed, and a plugin's as the plugin addressed it (`decision.plugin_addressed`). The feed says who passed it to whom (`decision.addressed`, with `deciders` and `decidersAdded`), and an agent account named anew is woken. For whoever builds a console: the client's `project(key).addressDecision(id, deciderIds)` (`PUT .../decisions/<id>/deciders`, `restDecisionDecidersSchema`); the data's `addressDecision`, `addressDecisionIn` and `membersOf`; `DecisionCard`, `DecisionList`, `TaskView`, `TaskThread`, `MyDecisions` and `ReachedDecision` take `addressing` (`DecisionAddressing`: the people to name, given or read when the form opens, and `onAddress`); the words `decisions.changeDeciders`, `addressNobody`, `addressHint` and `peopleNotLoaded`, and `feed.event.decisionAddressed`.
