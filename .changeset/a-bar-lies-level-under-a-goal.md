On the month's calendar, a goal's mark no longer pushes the bars of its own day a row down: every day of the week keeps the rows the day with the most goals has, so that a task lying across the week is one level line and its title crosses neither the tasks of the days after nor the goal itself. The count beside the workers' pictures ("+2") stays on one line where a row is narrow.
