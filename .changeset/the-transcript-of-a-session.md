The transcript of an agent account's sessions, on the console's side (ADR-0086). Its runner begins and ends a session (`POST /api/v1/agents/me/sessions`, `.../<id>/end`), reports its events in numbered batches that may be sent twice (`.../<id>/events`) and hands in the pictures its tools return (`.../<id>/files`); its supervisors and the account read the sessions and their events a page at a time and as they happen (`GET /api/v1/agents/<handle>/sessions`, `.../<id>/events?after=`, `.../<id>/events/stream`), and an administrator seals an event that must not be kept, on the record. Everything of a session is kept under the month it began in, apart from the board, so that whoever keeps the database moves a past month alone; a session whose month is away is still listed and says so. A run the account's command begins while a session is open names it (`sessionId`). For whoever builds a console: `restAgentSessionSchema`, `restAgentSessionsSchema`, `restAgentSessionInputSchema`, `restAgentSessionEndInputSchema`, `restTranscriptEventSchema`, `restTranscriptEventsSchema`, `restTranscriptEventInputSchema`, `restTranscriptBatchInputSchema`, `restTranscriptBatchSchema`, `restTranscriptFileSchema`, `restTranscriptTextSchema`, `restTranscriptSealInputSchema`, `SESSION_ENDS`, `TRANSCRIPT_KINDS`, `TRANSCRIPT_PICTURE_TYPES`, the bounds that begin with `MAX_TRANSCRIPT_`, and `ConsoleClient.agentSessions`, `agentSession`, `transcript`, `transcriptStreamUrl`, `transcriptText`, `transcriptFileUrl`, `sealTranscriptEvent`, `beginAgentSession`, `sendTranscript`, `uploadTranscriptFile` and `endAgentSession`.
