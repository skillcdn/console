Teams (ADR-0073): a team is a key, a name and its members, a person in any number of them, kept by an administrator on the People page's view of the teams (`/people/teams`) and with `console teams` and `console team new <key> "<name>"|rename|remove|add|drop`. A team is called by its key (ADR-0074): a project key's shape, given when the team is made and never changed, and one among the teams' keys and the projects' keys of the workspace, so that a project is refused a team's key (`project.key_taken`) as a team is refused a project's (`team.key_taken`). `GET /api/v1/teams` lists them with the members the asker may see; `POST /api/v1/teams`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /api/v1/teams/<key>` and `POST|DELETE /api/v1/teams/<key>/members` keep them, and the client's `teams`, `createTeam`, `renameTeam`, `removeTeam`, `addTeamMember` and `removeTeamMember` call them; the people listing and `ctx.console.people` name each person's teams by key (`teams`), and `ctx.console.teams.list()` and `get(key)` read the teams, with `people:read`. A role of a plugin is granted to a team by its key (`team` on `POST .../grants`, `--team <key>`, the settings page), held by whoever is in it as the membership changes, and a grant answers the team as `{ key, name }`. Five new event kinds, `team.created`, `team.renamed`, `team.removed`, `team.member_added` and `team.member_removed`, each with the team's `key`, reach a plugin's hooks with `people:read:all` as the people's do. The fact the People page called a person's team is their direct reports now.
