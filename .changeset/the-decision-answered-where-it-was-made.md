A choice made elsewhere reaches the record: `console answer <id> --option <n>|--own "<answer>" [--note <markdown>|--file <path>]` writes a person's answer on a decision in their name through the agent, as they gave it in chat, in a meeting or by changing the task, with where and when in the note, so that the board shows as waiting only what is still open and a run that waited goes on. The skill working-the-board (1.25), its terms and the console's own rules say what a decision is, a record of a choice that is a person's to make wherever they make it, that an answer given elsewhere is written there before the work goes on, and that a briefing starts from the decisions waiting for the person's answer.
