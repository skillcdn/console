A decision reaches whom it is addressed to wherever it is ([ADR-0068](https://github.com/skillcdn/console/blob/main/docs/adr/0068-a-decision-reaches-whom-it-is-addressed-to-wherever-it-is-and-a-plugins-reaches-the-managers-above-them.md)). A person finds a decision addressed to them among their decisions and on their plate in a project they may see or not, reads it at `GET /api/v1/decisions/<id>` (`{ project, decision, mayAnswer }`, with `?wait=` as the project's route has it) and answers it at `POST /api/v1/decisions/<id>/answer`; the pages show one at `/decisions/<id>`, and My decisions leads there for a project the person may not see. A plugin's decision may address anyone active of the workspace, not only who may work in its board project, and reaches whoever manages a decider, up the reporting line, to read and answer; a person's own decision still names people of the project. `console decision <id>` and `console answer <id>` read the project the command works in first and then the decision wherever it is, so that an agent waits for a plugin's decision from any checkout, and a plugin command that raised one says the project it waits in (`project` on the invocation, read as optional). The client gains `decision`, `awaitDecision` and `answerDecision` at the workspace, `createConsole` the page, and a console built on the package reads an older console as before. A role of a plugin may follow the reporting line: `follows: "reports"` on a role in the manifest, held by whoever someone active reports to, granted by nobody; `declaredRoles` answers `follows` (read as optional), `ctx.console.people.granted(role)` lists them, and the settings page says so.
