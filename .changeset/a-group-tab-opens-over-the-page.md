A group tab in the header opens its list of plugins over the page: the list was clipped by the row of tabs, which scrolls sideways, and could be neither seen nor pressed wherever a group held two plugins. The list is a popover in the top layer, placed under its tab with the names lined up under the tab's and within the window, measured as it opens, dismissed by a click elsewhere or the escape key, its caret turned while it is open; it names each plugin alone, the one the page is on in the accent. A mouse resting on the tab opens the list, and leaving the tab and the list closes it; a mouse's click on the tab goes where the tab leads, the group's first page; a finger, a pen or a key opens and closes the list by a press. The person's menu lists a group's plugins one by one under the group's name, where it led to the first alone. A list, or the person's menu, longer than the window has room for scrolls within itself, so that its last items, Sign out among them, are always reached; both close when the window loses the focus, as it does to a click in a plugin's frame, which the page never sees; and where the browser has no popover (Safari before 17) the tab shows and hides the list itself. A tab's name wider than the row has room for stops with an ellipsis, and the tab's title says it whole, Hangul counted as running about twice as wide. The menus are drawn on the raised surface, with a shadow of their own (`--sc-shadow-menu`, in `CONSOLE_TOKENS`).
