The Agents tab is in views, as the People tab and the settings are: the agent accounts that report to whoever looks, directly or through someone who does, at `/agents`, with every other agent account of the workspace under a heading of its own there for an administrator; and the agents that act as the person, each with a token of theirs, at `/agents?view=connected`, with the token made by hand and the way to connect one. An agent account's card says how it stands, what it is doing now and since when, and what it runs, and names its manager where that is someone else; the login it signs in with is said on its page. The People tab lists the people and then the agent accounts, each under a heading with how many, and an account's name and a way at the foot of its card lead to its page. For whoever builds a console: `agentsHref`, `AGENTS_VIEWS` and `AgentsView`; the route `agents` carries its `view`; `AgentAccountList` takes `me`; `PeopleList` takes `agentHref`.
