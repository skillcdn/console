A badge's tone says one thing: neutral for what merely is, accent (blue) for what is at work, success (green) for what is done, warning (amber) for what wants attention within the week, point (orange) for what presses today or is someone's, danger (red) for what is late or failed. The warning badge is a tint like the others rather than an outline; the point and warning colours are an orange and an amber a reader tells apart, each above 4.5:1 on its tint. An administrator and an owner are marked in the accent, not the warm point; the days to a due date darken as they shorten: amber within the week, orange today or tomorrow, red once past.
