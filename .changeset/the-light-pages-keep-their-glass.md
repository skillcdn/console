The light pages keep the glass of SkillCDN's look: the field takes colour, a periwinkle sky with the brand's blue low and wide and a larger blue-violet light high behind the panes (`--sc-color-sky`, `--sc-color-far*`, `--sc-color-light-*`, `--sc-light-*`), and a pane is translucent white with a bright rim, light along its top edge and a soft blue shadow (`--sc-color-glass*`, `--sc-glass-sheen`, `--sc-glass-lift`, `--sc-glass-lens`), the sheets inside it frosted white. Token values only; the text on a pane still reads at 4.5:1 where the field is at its deepest, `--sc-color-text-faint` a shade darker for it.
