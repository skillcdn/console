An agent account takes a supervisor's word at the messenger for what a tell is worth, in naming an account as theirs too (ADR-0172). Where the console does not say whose an account at the messenger is, a session's first words named a supervisor's tell as the one word that tells it, beside the address the messenger keeps; an agent asked its supervisor for a tell after they had said, from an account it knew to be theirs, that a second account is theirs as well. The first words now say that the supervisor's own word tells it, by a tell or at the messenger in an account that is theirs by the wake's line or by its address; never a name, what the account says of itself or what anyone else says, and an account known only by such a word names no other. Read by a runner from its next session on; the skill `running-an-agent` says the same to the agent and to whoever supervises it.
