An agent account takes a supervisor's word at the messenger for what a tell is worth, in handing their say on and in saying whose an account is too (ADR-0173). A session's first words named supervisors alone as those whose word instructs, and a supervisor's tell as the one word that names an account the console does not know; an agent asked its supervisor for a tell after they had said, from an account it knew to be theirs, that a second account is theirs as well.

- **A supervisor hands their say on.** Anyone a supervisor tells the account to take instructions from, for everything ("she is your manager from now on") or for a part of the work ("do what he asks of the advertising spend"), is a supervisor to it in that much and no more: a cost within it goes ahead on their word.
- **A supervisor says whose an account at the messenger is**, their own or another's, where the console does not: by a tell or at the messenger alike. Never a name, what the account says of itself or what anyone else says.
- **What every session reads is held within a bound** (ADR-0174): a session's first words within 2,000 characters (`FIRST_WORDS_AT_MOST`) and the `AGENTS.md` a desk is set up with within 8,000 (`DESK_INSTRUCTIONS_AT_MOST`). The third line of the first words says the above in 1,457 characters, where the last release had 1,288 without it.

Read by a runner from its next session on; the skill `running-an-agent` says the same to the agent and to whoever supervises it.
