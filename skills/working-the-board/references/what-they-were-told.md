# What the person was told

`console notifications` lists what the board told the person, `--unread` what they have not read; reading it marks nothing, and nothing is marked read (`--read`) but on their word. A task's notifications are turned on or off for them when they say so: `console task follow <number>` turns them on, which has a task their settings would be silent of tell them of everything on it, and `console task mute <number>` turns them off; `console task <number>` says how they stand.
