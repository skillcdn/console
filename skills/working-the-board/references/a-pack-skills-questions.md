# A pack skill's own questions

A skill of the pack the work is done with has a "How the person is involved" of its own, and work that is on the board skips none of it. What that skill asks before it begins, its inputs (what is sold and at what price, which account, which language), is asked as the skill says, in one message, in chat where the person who gave the work is in the conversation. What they answer is written on the task before anything is made, `console comment <number>` with their words as theirs, so that the board holds what the work was built on. What that skill has a person approve on the way, a plan, a cost, a step that is not taken back, is a decision on the board (phase 4), with the skill's choices as its options; an answer the person gives in chat is written on it as theirs. With nobody in the conversation the inputs are asked on the board too: one decision for what the work cannot begin without, what the skill would assume among its options, and the work that does not depend on the answer goes on.
