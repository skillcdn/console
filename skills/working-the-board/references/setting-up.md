# Setting up, when told to

A task or a person may ask for the project itself rather than work in it: a project made or changed (`console project new <key> "<name>"`, `console project edit <key>`), its mark (`console project icon <key> <file>`), its members (`console member add <login>`, `console member role`, `console member remove`), the workspace's categories (`console category new <key> "<name>"`, `console category edit`, `console category remove`), what a person is (`console person role <login> <admin|member>`), its goals (`console goal new`), and its rules (`console rules write --file <path>`, from [the example](project-rules.md); the workspace's with `--workspace`, for an administrator). The agent does these as its person may, an owner of the project or an administrator of the workspace ([ADR-0021](/docs/adr/0021-a-token-configures-as-its-person-does.md)); the feed names the agent beside the person on each. What the person may not, the console refuses (`auth.forbidden`): that step goes back to the person in the report, never worked around. `console people` and `console members` say who is who, by login.
