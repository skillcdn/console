# Setting up, when told to

A task or a person may ask for the project itself rather than work in it: a project made or changed (`console project new <key> "<name>"`, `console project edit <key>`), its mark (`console project icon <key> <file>`), its members (`console member add <login>`, `console member role`, `console member remove`), the workspace's categories (`console category new <key> "<name>"`, `console category edit`, `console category remove`), what a person is (`console person role <login> <admin|member>`), its goals (`console goal new`), and its rules (`console rules write --file <path>`, from [the example](project-rules.md); the workspace's with `--workspace`, for an administrator). The agent does these as its person may, an owner of the project or an administrator of the workspace ([ADR-0021](/docs/adr/0021-a-token-configures-as-its-person-does.md)); the feed names the agent beside the person on each. What the person may not, the console refuses (`auth.forbidden`): that step goes back to the person in the report, never worked around. `console people` and `console members` say who is who, by login; `console workspace` says the workspace's settings, and `console workspace edit` changes them for an administrator: `--people-visibility <everyone|shared-projects>`, who sees whom on the People page; `--week-start <auto|monday|sunday>`, the day the calendars begin a week on; `--time-zone <zone|none>`, the workspace's time zone, an IANA name such as `Asia/Seoul`, in which the command prints moments ([ADR-0035](/docs/adr/0035-who-may-see-whom-is-decided-by-one-policy-and-the-workspace-keeps-its-settings.md)). In a project open to the workspace everyone is a member already: `console member add <login> --role owner` lists an owner, and `member role <login> member` takes one back; a plain `member add` is refused there (`member.open_project`). A login is found exactly, so an owner lists someone `console people` does not show them. A private pack is connected by an owner with `console project pack-token <key> --connect` ([ADR-0036](/docs/adr/0036-a-private-pack-is-connected-with-a-code-a-person-approves-from-the-projects-settings.md)): the command prints a page at SkillCDN and a code, which the agent relays; the person approves there, signed in as someone who may open the repository, and the console keeps the token, shown to nobody. Until then `console skills` says the pack is not served to the console.
