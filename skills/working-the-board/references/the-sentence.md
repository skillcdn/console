# The sentence a repository carries

A repository whose checkouts are worked through a console says so in its agreement (AGENTS.md, or CLAUDE.md where that is the file agents read), in one sentence copied as it is and placed at the head of its workflow; a pack says it in the rules of its `SKILLCDN.md`, with `the checkout` for `this checkout`. The sentence binds the checkout to the console and says nothing of how: the command's help and the skill working-the-board say how, and the repository's own agreement keeps saying how a change is committed and pushed.

> **Bound to a console?** When `.skillcdn-console.json` is in this checkout, or `console whoami` names a project, work the task through that SkillCDN console (`console help` says how).
