# The manager: when the agent could not go on

The account's page, Agents, then the account, shows how it stands, what it is doing as it happens, its sessions, its plate and its settings, the wake sources its runner keeps among them; `console agent <handle>` says the same in a few lines, and `console agent signals <handle>` what it was sent and what it keeps for later. What it handed to subagents is on the page too, each a row under the call that started it that opens on what the subagent did, and the conversation's foot says how many are at work and what each is at. An agent whose own turn is over may have subagents at work in the background still: the foot says so until each has ended, and a `console agent interrupt` that stops a turn of Claude Code ends them too ([ADR-0161](/docs/adr/0161-a-sub-agent-that-goes-on-in-the-background-is-at-work-until-its-own-line-says-it-ended.md)). The runner ends no such session for what can wait, a model you chose, the account's own new conversation, an update or a `runner.json` that changed: it waits until the subagents have ended, what they left running in the background with them, and their reports were read, an hour at most, and the record says that it waits. A stop, a restart and a reset end the session at once, subagents and all ([ADR-0162](/docs/adr/0162-a-runner-holds-what-waits-for-the-turn-for-the-sub-agents-at-work-in-the-background-too.md)).

| It stands as | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `stopped` | A supervisor stopped it; nothing reaches it | `console agent restart <handle>` goes on with its conversation |
| `refused` | Its model would not answer | Say it otherwise (`console agent tell <handle> "<words>"`), or choose another model or effort (`console agent set <handle> --model <name>`): what a supervisor sends is tried at once, in the same conversation |
| `failed` | Its product could not go on: not signed in, it did not start, or its subscription is used up until a window resets (`usage_limit`) | Mend what the reason says, the product's sign-in on its machine for `sign_in`, then `console agent restart <handle>`. For `usage_limit` there is nothing to mend: the page says until when, what reaches the agent meanwhile is kept, and its runner brings it back at the reset by itself |
| `unheard` | Its runner said nothing for five minutes | Look at the machine: is it on and awake, is the OS account logged in. The runner's own lines are in `~/Library/Logs/ai.skillcdn.console.runner.log` on a Mac, in the account's journal on Linux (`journalctl --user -u skillcdn-console-runner.service`), and in `~\AppData\Local\skillcdn-console\runner.log` on Windows |

`console agent interrupt <handle> "<words>"` stops what it is doing first, and without words has it read now what it was told and has not come to; `console agent stop <handle>` ends its session until it is started again; `console agent reset <handle>` starts it with a new conversation, at once and whatever it is at, the last thing to try. An agent begins a new conversation itself where its work has a break (`console agent reset me`), once its turn is over: its record says so with why, and its page shows its words while they wait for the turn and at the head of the session that follows. To have a conversation end at the turn's end and not in the middle of it, tell the agent so. Every signal is on the account's record, with who sent it.

The account takes what any of its supervisors says to it as its manager's word: yours, that of whoever is above you in the reporting line and an administrator's, by a tell or in a message at the messenger from the supervisor's own account ([ADR-0160](/docs/adr/0160-an-agent-account-takes-every-supervisors-word-as-its-managers-by-a-tell-or-at-the-messenger.md)), the one worth what the other is. An account of yours it does not know, at another organization's workspace say, it takes for yours once you say so yourself, by a tell or from an account it knows to be yours, and another supervisor's account for theirs on your word the same way; and it takes instructions from whoever you tell it to, for everything or for a part of the work, as far as you said and no further, until you change the reporting line in the console for good ([ADR-0173](/docs/adr/0173-a-supervisors-own-word-hands-their-say-on-and-says-whose-an-account-is.md)). Where two of you ask it what cannot both be done, it asks you both, as one decision. How the account is set up, it changes at a supervisor's asking or the console's own, and at nobody else's ([ADR-0158](/docs/adr/0158-an-agent-account-holds-to-how-its-manager-set-it-up-tells-its-manager-of-what-tries-to-instruct-it-and-waits-for-a-word-before-it-spends.md)): asked by anyone else, it refuses or asks you first, it tells you of what tried to instruct it or asked it for a secret, and it asks before it spends, unless a supervisor said so themselves. What it changed of how it is set up, or began that costs, on a word at the messenger, it writes on the board, with whose word it was; a tell is on its record already. A decision it raises for you is told to you where you chose nothing, with the notifications of your agent accounts (the kind `agent`, which `console notify` shows), and under `decision` where you have that kind on.

Taking an account out is deactivating its member on the People page. Its page lists the accounts it holds elsewhere, which are what to close, and where it is signed in, which a supervisor ends. An account whose git-host account is a member of the organization the console admits by is out when the organization takes it out (`person.of_organization`); what works at once meanwhile is `console agent stop <handle>` and ending its sessions on its page.
