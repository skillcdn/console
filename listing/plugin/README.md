# SkillCDN AI Team

Hire AI employees on one board. This plugin is the guide to [SkillCDN Console](https://github.com/skillcdn/console), the open-source console where AI employees work beside your people.

An AI employee joins your team with an account of its own. It takes work from the board, reports as it goes, hands in what it made, and asks only what is yours to decide. You put the work on the board, and just decide.

## What you can ask

- "What is SkillCDN Console, and what do I need to try it on my computer?"
- "Walk me through hiring my first AI employee, from what to prepare."
- "What does it take to set up a console for my team at our own address?"
- "How does an AI employee ask me for a decision, and where do I answer it?"
- "I want our console to track customers. How do I write a plugin for that?"

Your AI answers from the console's own skills, step-by-step guides an AI follows, and its documentation, as they stand today.

## What it is made of

- **One connection**, to `https://skillcdn.ai/gh/skillcdn/console`: the console's skills and documents, served by [SkillCDN](https://skillcdn.ai), which hands the skills kept in a git repository to the AI app you already use. It has four tools, all of them read-only: search, load a skill, read a file, browse a folder. It asks for no account and no sign-in.
- **One skill**, `get-started`, which tells your AI to read the pack's first page and to follow the skill that page names.

## What it does and does not do

It reads. It sends that connection the words of a search and the path of a file it asks for, and nothing else of your conversation; SkillCDN's [privacy policy](https://skillcdn.ai/privacy) says what its server keeps. It runs no program, installs nothing, and changes nothing on your computer.

It does not connect to your board. Bringing a console up, signing in and deploying take commands on your computer, which a coding agent there carries out, such as Claude Code or Codex. The guide says how to connect one and what to tell it.
