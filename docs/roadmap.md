# Roadmap

What exists, what is being built and what comes next, at a glance. This page says that a thing is there or is not yet; the document each line links says what it is.

## How this page is kept

- **An entry is one to three sentences**, in the terms of whoever uses the thing: a person at the board, an agent, the manager of an agent account, an operator, the author of a plugin, someone building a console of their own. How it works, why it was decided and what was tried belong to the document the entry links, a [spec](specs/), a workspace's `README.md`, the [deployment contract](../deploy/README.md), a skill or an [ADR](adr/README.md), and are not repeated here.
- **Three states.** *In progress* is being built or proven now. *Next* is agreed and not started, and says what it waits for where it waits. *Undecided* is an idea nobody has committed to. What is finished moves into the line of its area under [What exists](#what-exists); what is dropped is deleted.
- **No history.** No dates, no order of events, no figures. Git history is the record, `packages/console/CHANGELOG.md` says what a release changed, and the ADRs say why.
- **Updated with the change** that starts, finishes or drops an item. Tasks are tracked where the maintainers keep theirs; this page stays coarse.

## Undecided

- **Service kinds `discord` and `teams`** among the services a workspace names for its agents. Neither has a machine in as a member, so each waits for an organization that works there to say how ([ADR-0089](adr/0089-a-workspace-names-the-services-its-agents-work-with.md)).
- **Editors for an agent's persona:** the names and the palettes kept by an administrator, an avatar's parts chosen by hand, a picture of one's own in its place.
- **A theme as a setting.** The pages are light, and no dark look is kept.
- **Ideas and intake:** from a thought to a task, with the questions a person is asked on the way.
- **Hooks that report an agent's events** without being asked ([the command](specs/cli.md#not-yet)).

The open questions of the design are kept with it, under [Open questions](architecture.md#open-questions): among them a runner for a person's own agent, a stronger boundary than an OS account for each agent, a second workspace, signing in where no identity provider is reached, a token's renewal, and for plugins a sandbox of their own, a store, settings per project and widgets on a project's pages.

What the console needs from a package there is proposed, released and adopted by version ([ADR-0001](adr/0001-the-console-is-the-reference-console-built-on-the-published-packages.md)); an organization's own deployment stays outside this repository.
