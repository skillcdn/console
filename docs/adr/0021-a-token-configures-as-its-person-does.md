# ADR-0021: A token configures as its person does

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-11

## Context

A token is its person on the board ([ADR-0004](0004-people-and-agents-reach-the-board-only-through-the-api-with-a-credential-of-their-own.md), [ADR-0008](0008-a-workspace-holds-projects-and-what-a-person-may-see-and-change-is-decided-per-project.md)): what the person may see and change in a project, their agent may, and every event names the agent beside the person. Configuring was the exception: making a project, changing its settings or its image, listing its members, keeping the categories, saying what a person is and keeping the person's language were a person's own doing, signed in on the console's own pages, never with a token, and the pages of a console of one's own were built on that ([ADR-0014](0014-a-persons-own-console-is-served-from-their-machine-by-the-command.md), point 4). The first organization to run on a deployment of its own sent an agent to set up a workspace for its person, and the agent stopped at every one of these and handed the person a list of clicks. The rule protected nothing the roles did not protect already: an owner's agent was refused what a member's agent was refused, and a token is scoped to one person, revocable, and expiring unless the person chose otherwise. What the rule did was make an agent useless for the first hour of a project.

## Decision

1. **Every request a session may make, a token may make, except the tokens themselves.** `GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /api/v1/tokens`, `GET` and `DELETE /api/v1/people/<id>/tokens`, and the reading, approval and denial of an agent's connection, which makes a token, stay a person's own doing, signed in on the console's own pages (`auth.session_required`), so that a token which leaks cannot outlive its removal through tokens of its own. Everything else, configuring included (a project made, changed or given its image; its members; the categories; what a person is; the person's choice of language; giving up on anyone's run as an owner), a token does as its person does, with the role checks exactly as they are: an administrator, an owner, a member.
2. **The attribution stays.** What a token writes names the agent beside the person: `project.created`, `project.updated`, `project.member_added`, `project.member_changed`, `project.member_removed`, `category.created`, `category.updated`, `category.removed` and `person.role_changed`, as every other event does.
3. **The command has the words for it.** `people`, `person role`, `project new`, `project edit`, `project icon`, `members`, `member add`, `member role`, `member remove`, `category new`, `category edit` and `category remove`, under "Configuring, as the person" in `console help`, each with its limits and its refusals by code. The tokens themselves have no command.
4. **The pages follow.** A console of a person's own, served on a token ([ADR-0014](0014-a-persons-own-console-is-served-from-their-machine-by-the-command.md)), offers what the organization's console offers on a session, the Agents pages and the approval of a connection excepted, and keeps the choice of language on the person rather than in the browser. This amends point 4 of ADR-0014.

## Consequences

- An agent sets up the project it is told to, as far as its person may, and the person reads on the board who did what and through which agent. An agent told to do what its person may not is refused with `auth.forbidden`, as the person would be, and hands that step to them.
- A token that leaks can do in configuring what its person can: open a private project to the workspace, list or remove members, change a role. The scope is the workspace, whose people the operator lists; the token expires unless the person chose otherwise, is removed on the Agents page by the person or an administrator, and what it did is in the feed under its name. The rule that a token cannot make tokens is what bounds the damage, and it stands.
- Rejected, for now: a grant on the token ("may configure"), given on the approval page and on `POST /api/v1/tokens`, with a token without it refused as before (`configure.not_granted`). It would be a second role system beside the roles, every agent would ask for it, and a person approving their own agent would grant it every time. Should an organization need a token that works the board but never configures, this is the shape to add.
