# ADR-0150: A runner waits out its product's usage limit, and tells its agent before it

- Status: Accepted; point 3 is amended by [ADR-0166](0166-a-wake-carries-what-the-agents-conversation-does-not-hold-and-says-it-once.md): what the product took and the agent never answered is named when it is handed over again, and not said a second time
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Amends [ADR-0085](0085-a-runner-on-the-agents-machine-is-told-by-signals-and-runs-one-session-at-a-time.md), by which a runner restarts nothing by itself and an agent that cannot go on waits for a supervisor. The runner is in [specs/runner.md](../specs/runner.md).

## Context

An agent's product runs on a subscription that allows so much within a window that resets, a short one and the week for Claude Code. A product that has used a window up refuses every request until the reset, of the session and of each sub-agent alike, since they draw on the one allowance. The driver read such a turn as a turn that ended: the account stood as `running`, what reached the agent meanwhile was handed to a product that refused it and was counted as delivered, and after the reset nothing brought the agent back but the next thing someone sent. Before the limit the agent knew nothing either, unless it asked its product, so it stopped in the middle of its work having told nobody.

## Decision

1. **A usage limit is a reason of its own, with a moment.** A driver that reads its product refusing on the limit says `cannot_go_on` with `failed`, `usage_limit` and, where the product says so, when the limit resets. The heartbeat carries the reason and the moment (`until`), the console keeps both with the account, and the account's page, `console agent <handle>` and `agent.state_changed` say them.
2. **The runner brings the agent back by itself.** This is the one state it ends without a supervisor, since waiting is all that cures it. A minute after the moment the product named it hands the agent a message of its own: that the limit stopped it and from when, that its turn and its sub-agents ended where they were, and what reached it meanwhile. Where the product named no moment the runner tries after the cool-down, longer each time it is refused. It looks for the moment by the clock at every heartbeat too, since a timer rings late over a sleep. The limit is kept in the runner's state file, so a runner started meanwhile brings the agent back too.
3. **Nothing is lost meanwhile.** What arrives by itself is kept, not handed to a product that refuses it. What the agent was handed and never answered when the limit stopped it, taken by the product or not, is handed over again. What the product took is delivered by the record, so the runner writes its number in its state file, and a runner started meanwhile takes it from the stream once more. A supervisor's signals are tried at once, as for any agent that cannot go on, and alone: the rest stays kept and undelivered. A `restart` starts the agent whatever the limit said.
4. **The agent goes on where it was stopped.** A return never begins a new conversation, whatever `conversation` in `runner.json` says of a new day: what the agent was doing is in the one it was stopped in.
5. **The agent stands as stopped until it answers.** Not when it is handed the message, which a limit that still stands refuses: when it says or does something again.
6. **Before the limit, the runner tells the agent, and adds no rule.** A driver says how much of each window is used whenever its product says so (`usage`), and says nothing where its product does not. At 80% and at 90% of a window the runner tells the agent once (a window a driver says apart, a quota kept for another model than the session's, is shown and tells nothing), under its own header: how much of which window is used, when it resets, and what the limit does. Of a window of a part of the allowance, one model's week, it says what the window counts and no more: what the agent does about a model's limit is its own to decide. At once, in the open session: in the turn under way where there is one, and in a turn of its own where there is none, since sub-agents left at work spend the allowance after the session's turn ended, and a word kept for the next message may come after the limit. The line carries what there is to know, and no template, skill or rule says anything of it ([ADR-0146](0146-the-desks-template-spends-no-sentence-on-what-an-agent-does-by-itself.md)).
7. **The allowance is read from the product's own stream.** Claude Code writes it after a request, as numbers (seen with 2.1.330). Nothing is asked of the product for it and nothing it prints for people is parsed.

## Consequences

- An account stopped by its limit shows as `failed` with why and until when, and comes back with nobody's help. A plugin that tells people reads the moment from the event.
- **A console of before the word refuses a heartbeat that says it.** The runner then says `crashed`, the nearest word that console takes, and no moment, so that the account is still heard from; no floor moves.
- **Telling the agent of its allowance may cost a turn**, where none was under way: two for a window at most.
- **Codex and a product over `acp` say nothing of their allowance**, as far as their drivers read them: the agent is told nothing before the limit, and the limit itself is read from the words of an error, with no moment. Neither was seen with a product whose allowance was used up, and neither was Claude Code's stream of a limit reached, which is written down from the product's own record of one.
- Rejected: **a state of its own** beside `failed`, which an older command's reader refuses, for a word on a badge; **asking the product for its usage on a timer** (`claude -p "/usage"` costs no request, and prints a text for people whose format is no contract), while the stream says the same as numbers; **a wake the account keeps at the console for the reset** ([ADR-0145](0145-an-agent-account-wakes-itself-now-or-at-a-moment-to-come.md)), whose words are fixed when it is kept and cannot say what arrived meanwhile; **stopping the agent at a share of the window**, which is a supervisor's or the agent's own to decide; **a threshold in `runner.json`**, until someone needs another.
