# ADR-0097: An avatar is a planet of flat shapes in two tones, filled as pixels

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-12
- Supersedes [ADR-0093](0093-an-avatar-is-a-form-cut-from-a-material-drawn-as-pixels.md), which made the avatar a form cut from a material, a picture computed point by point. What that decision kept of point 8 of [ADR-0084](0084-an-agent-account-is-a-member-of-its-own-with-a-manager-above-it.md) stands: a seed and its parts, drawn where shown, a picture file where one is needed, a released set that never changes, nothing uploaded and no markup from outside. Its points 5 and 6 are taken over as they were: a page shows the picture as an image it made itself, and an agent account's picture is a soft square where a person's is round.

## Context

The still lifes of ADR-0093 were put to a design review, and the review asked for another direction. What it proposed in their place was a set drawn by a designer: a planet, flat and friendly, which stays one family however many accounts there are. A planet is a body, what lies on its surface, a ramp of colours, and one thing beside it or nothing. Every shape is in two tones of its ramp, the lighter left of a line down the picture and a step darker right of it, which gives a flat picture a volume without a shadow; what stands beside the body is drawn in a ramp chosen to go with the body's, so that no two colours meet by chance; and a few faint squares are strewn over a ground that is nearly white.

Such a set is outlines, as the first set of ADR-0084 was, and no function of a point. What ADR-0093 found still holds, though: a picture file is needed where there is no browser, since the command writes one; a page's policy takes an image the page made itself; and the same parts have to give the same pixels wherever they are drawn. So the outlines are not handed to a browser as SVG. The package fills them itself.

No set was ever released: no version of the package carried one, and no deployment holds an avatar made with one. So the set is replaced once more, with no second set beside one nobody has, and the parts keep their names and their shape, so that nothing of the REST contract moves.

## Decision

1. **An avatar is a small planet.** Its parts are the six numbers they were, each from 0 to 255, with `set`, the version of the set they are places in; what each is a place among is new. `form` is the body, `material` what lies on its surface, `palette` the ramp of colours both are drawn in, `accent` what stands beside the body, `0` for nothing, and `ground` the colour behind everything. `vein` is the seed the squares on the ground are strewn by and is a place in nothing: two avatars alike in every other part still differ in their squares.
2. **The first set** has four bodies (a disc, a smaller disc, a lumpy one, one with an atmosphere), ten surfaces (straight bands, wavy bands, zigzag bands, tilted bands, the eye of a storm, a great spot, continents, craters, polar caps, day and night), eighteen ramps of five tones from light to dark, each naming the ramp that goes with it, five things to stand beside the body (a ring, an orbit with a small moon on it, a moon, two moons, a sparkle) or none, and one ground. A surface is drawn across the whole picture and cut to the body, so any surface fits any body: 4,320 planets, each with 256 ways its squares fall. A body, a surface and a ramp are rolled alike; nothing stands beside the body a little less than half the time.
3. **A set is data: outlines in a square of a hundred units a side.** `AvatarSet` holds the grounds and the colours of the squares, the ramps, the bodies, the surfaces and the accents. A shape is outlines filled together, the tone of its ramp it takes left of its line (the next darker one right of it) or two colours of its own, where the line is, how much of what is behind it shows through, and a gap, a line of the ground's colour round it that sets it off what it lies over. Outlines that run the same way round add up to one shape, and one that runs the other way cuts a hole, as the inner oval of a ring does. What stands beside a body is layers behind it and before it, each turned about the middle of the picture and kept to its upper or its lower half where it says so: that is how a ring goes round a planet.
4. **The package fills the outlines itself, by how much of each pixel they cover.** `avatarPixels` computes for every pixel the share of it an outline goes round, and gives a pixel its two tones at once, each by its share, so that no line of what is behind shows where the tones meet. A body's surface is drawn whole and put on the picture through the body's outline, so that the body's edge is smoothed once. There is no SVG of an avatar and no renderer of anyone else's, as before.
5. **The same parts give the same pixels on every machine.** A curve is laid out as short sides, by a sine computed here from its series; everything is sums, products and square roots, and nothing a runtime may round its own way. A test holds the set by a digest of its data, every corner of every outline, and the drawing by a digest of what it draws, which is what "a released set never changes" means for a set of data and the function that fills it.
6. **A small picture is drawn for its size.** Under 96 pixels a side, which is a picture shown under 48, the ground has no squares: they would be specks. Under 64, a picture shown in a line of text or among a task's workers, the planet is drawn closer: as large as what stands beside it leaves room for, and no more than 1.4 times. The squares are the set's own rule; drawing the planet closer is the package's, for the sizes the set says nothing of.
7. **The tile's corners are rounded by a fifth of its side**, as the set is drawn, wherever the stylesheet cuts an agent account's picture. A picture file is the whole square, the ground to its corners: a profile elsewhere cuts a picture its own way.
8. **A set of a deployer's own is data again.** `createConsole`'s `persona.avatarSets` takes it as before, under a version the package does not use. What such a set holds that is no outline, no colour and no number is left out or drawn in black, and never fails; a place far outside the picture is brought back; and a picture is drawn from twenty thousand corners at most, some ten times what the first set's fullest takes, an outline past that being left out, so that the time a picture takes has a bound whatever a set holds.

## Consequences

- `restAvatarPartsSchema` is as it was, and the snapshot of the REST contract does not move. The names of the parts say less than they did: `material` names a surface and `vein` a seed of squares. They are kept, since a name is not worth a contract moved, and the schema says what each is a place among.
- The package exports `AVATAR_BOX`, `AVATAR_PLAIN_UNDER`, `AVATAR_CLOSE_UNDER`, `AVATAR_CLOSE_MOST` and the types of a set's data (`AvatarRamp`, `AvatarPoint`, `AvatarOutline`, `AvatarShape`, `AvatarLayer`, `AvatarBody`, `AvatarSurface`, and `AvatarAccent`, which was a name and is layers now). It no longer exports `AVATAR_FLOOR`, `AvatarPalette`, `AvatarColors`, `AvatarForm`, `AvatarMaterial` or `AvatarGround`. Whoever wrote a set for the still lifes writes it again.
- A picture file of the largest size is some tens of kilobytes, where a stone's was near a megabyte: flat tones pack. Drawing takes about a millisecond for a picture a page shows and some thirty for the largest, on a laptop.
- The pixels were held against the same outlines drawn by a browser as SVG, at 256 pixels a side, over the nine planets the set was shown with and sixty rolled ones: they differ by less than one part in 255 a pixel on the average, and by more than eight parts in half a percent of the pixels, all of them along edges, which the two smooth differently, and along the line where two tones meet, where a browser lets a hair of what is behind show and the package does not. The comparison needs a browser and is not part of `pnpm check`.
- The palest ramps stand little off a ground that is nearly white once a picture is as small as a chip. Whether the set is tuned for that before it is first released is its review's to say; after a release it would be a second set.
- What an editor of an avatar's parts would offer changes with the parts once more; none is built ([roadmap](../roadmap.md)).
